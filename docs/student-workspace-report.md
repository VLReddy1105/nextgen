## Architectural decisions

No automatic university verification was inferred from a typed institution or email domain. Existing enrollment/invitation authority is retained. Saved opportunities remain separate from submitted applications. Ratings/booking and AI automation are future scope. A discovered historical ownership-trigger bug was fixed through a new migration: community updates now branch before referencing project-only columns, while immutable ownership checks remain. Extended student career data and private resume references are restricted to the owner/admin; current non-student frontend queries do not read other students' detail rows.
